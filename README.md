Prototype Canteen Analysis Dashboard
Project Overview
The Prototype Canteen Analysis Dashboard is an HR analytics project
designed to evaluate employee attrition before and after the
introduction of a canteen facility.
The organization has a workforce of 2,000 employees across five
cities and is currently experiencing an attrition rate of
approximately 10%. Management introduced a canteen facility as an
employee-retention initiative, with an expected reduction in attrition
to approximately 7%.
The dashboard provides a data-driven view of attrition and helps HR
understand how the change varies across cities, employee roles, and
months.
Business Problem
The organization needs to evaluate whether employee attrition improved
after the canteen intervention and identify areas where attrition
remains relatively high.
The workforce distribution is:
City          Employees
---
Hyderabad           300
Bangalore           400
Pune                200
Mumbai              500
Chennai             600
Total     2,000
Objective
The objective is to develop an HR analytics dashboard that:
Compares attrition before and after the canteen intervention.
Analyzes monthly attrition trends.
Compares attrition across cities.
Compares attrition across employee roles.
Identifies higher-attrition city and role combinations.
Assesses the effectiveness of the canteen initiative.
Key KPIs
The dashboard includes the following key performance indicators:
Total Employees: 2,000
Current Attrition: Approximately 10%
Post-Canteen Attrition: Approximately 7%
Attrition Reduction: Approximately 3 percentage points
Dashboard Visualizations
1. KPI Cards
The KPI cards provide a quick overview of:
Total Employees
Current Attrition %
Post-Canteen Attrition %
Attrition Reduction
2. City and Job Role Slicers
Interactive slicers allow users to filter the dashboard by:
City

3. Workforce Distribution
A donut chart provides a quick view of the workforce distribution across
the five cities.
4. Monthly Attrition Trend
A line chart compares current attrition with post-canteen attrition
across the 12 months.
The dashboard shows that attrition is approximately 10% before the
intervention and approximately 7% after the intervention, with the
pattern varying across the months.
5. City-wise Attrition Analysis
A clustered column chart compares current and post-canteen attrition
across:
Chennai
Hyderabad
Bangalore
Mumbai
Pune
Attrition declined across all five cities after the canteen
intervention. The dashboard identifies Pune as having the highest
post-canteen attrition in the documented analysis.
6. Role-wise Attrition Analysis
A clustered column chart compares attrition across:
WM Technician
AC Technician
RF Technician
Helper
Key findings:
WM Technicians had the highest pre-canteen attrition at
10.20%.
Their attrition decreased to 7.00% after the intervention.
AC Technicians have the highest post-canteen attrition at
7.24%.
All four roles experienced a reduction in attrition after the
canteen intervention.
This indicates a consistent positive association between the canteen
initiative and employee retention.
7. City × Role Attrition Matrix
The matrix compares attrition across combinations of city and employee

Key Insights
Overall Attrition
Overall attrition decreased from approximately 10% to 7% after the
canteen intervention, representing a 3 percentage-point reduction.
City-wise Insight
Attrition declined across all five cities after the intervention. Pune
was identified as having the highest post-canteen attrition in the
dashboard analysis.
Role-wise Insight
WM Technicians had the highest attrition before the intervention at
10.20%. After the intervention, their attrition fell to 7.00%.
AC Technicians have the highest remaining post-canteen attrition at
7.24%, making this role a potential area for additional retention
attention.
Consistency of Canteen Impact
The reduction in attrition is observed across all four employee roles.
This consistent reduction suggests a positive association between the
canteen initiative and employee retention.
> **Important:** The dashboard demonstrates an association between the
> canteen intervention and lower attrition. It does not, by itself,
> establish that the canteen directly caused the reduction in attrition.
Future Analysis
The project can be extended by adding canteen usage and cost-related
analysis, including:
How much the canteen is being used.
Total company spending on the canteen.
Canteen spending per employee.
Average number of meals consumed by each employee.
Whether canteen usage is increasing or decreasing over the 12
months.
Which city uses the canteen the most.
Tools / Technology
Power BI -- Dashboard development and visualization
DAX -- KPI and attrition calculations
Data Analysis -- City, role, and monthly attrition analysis
Dashboard Outcome
The dashboard provides management and HR with an interactive view of
employee attrition and the change observed after the canteen
intervention. It helps identify high-risk employee groups and provides a
foundation for further analysis of canteen usage, costs, and employee
retention.
